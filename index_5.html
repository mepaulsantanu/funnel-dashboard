    What happens to your file

    1. Read

      Finds the real header, even under title rows, and skips totals and notes.

    2. Repair

      Turns "₹1,23,456" and "5 Aug 2026" into numbers and dates that add up.

    3. Standardise

      Merges "fb ads", "Meta Ads" and "paid social" into one channel.

    4. Fill and flag

      Fills gaps, removes duplicates and flags outliers with the IQR rule.

    5. Build

      Draws the funnel, channels, trends and statistics, ready to filter.
